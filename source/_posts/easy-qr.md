## 1. Introduction

QRaft ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the QRaft mobile application.

By using QRaft, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive:

- **Unique user ID** (from Google or Apple)
- **Email address** (if you grant permission)
- **Display name** (if provided by the identity provider)
- **Profile picture URL** (Google only, if provided)

We do not receive your password. Authentication is handled entirely by Google or Apple.

### 2.2 Content You Create

- QR codes you generate, including their content, type, and associated settings
- Files or images you attach to QR codes (uploaded to cloud storage)

### 2.3 Device Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | Scanning QR codes and capturing photos for QR code content |
| **Photo Library** | Selecting images from your device to embed in or attach to QR codes |
| **Internet** | Communicating with our servers to sync your QR codes and account data |

We do not access your camera or photo library without an explicit action from you (e.g., tapping "Scan" or "Choose Photo").

### 2.4 Purchase Information

If you subscribe to QRaft Pro, purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We receive a **transaction receipt** to verify your subscription status. We do **not** receive or store your payment card details.

### 2.5 Technical Data

- Device type and operating system version (for debugging and compatibility)
- App version
- Error logs (anonymous, used to fix crashes)

We do **not** collect precise location data, contacts, or any data unrelated to QR code management.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Legal Basis |

| Verify subscription status (IAP receipts) | Contract performance |

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on servers hosted by **Aiven (PostgreSQL)** and **Cloudflare R2** (file storage).
- All data in transit is encrypted using **TLS/HTTPS**.
- Access tokens use short-lived JWTs; refresh tokens are stored as hashed values.
- We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.

## 6. Children's Privacy

QRaft is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access** – Request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Correction** – Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion** – Request deletion of your account and associated data
- **Portability** – Request an export of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email below.

---

## 8. Data Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account exists.
- QR codes and associated files are retained until you delete them or your account.
- After account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days.
